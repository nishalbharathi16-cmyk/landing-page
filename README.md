# 🏛️ LAMA · Leadership & Managerial Skills Academy
### Microsoft Fluent 2 & Copilot-Inspired Executive Finishing 3D Landing Page

An applied executive finishing academy landing page for **LAMA**, modeled after **Microsoft Fluent 2 design language**, **Microsoft Learn**, and **Microsoft Copilot / Azure enterprise architectures**.

---

## 🌟 Microsoft Design Highlights

- **Microsoft Fluent & Acrylic Aesthetics**:
  - Precision grid alignment, soft natural elevations (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-md`), and 1px hairline boundaries (`border-slate-200`).
  - Microsoft Azure Blue (`#0078D4` / `#005A9E`), Royal Cobalt (`#104E92`), and LAMA Terracotta (`#C87549`) accents.
- **3D Armillary Knowledge Nexus (`LamaAcademy3D.jsx`)**:
  - Translucent crystalline diamond core with rotating Azure and brushed brass concentric coordinate rings.
  - Interactive satellite beacons for Domain 01 (Azure Blue), Domain 02 (Brushed Gold), and Domain 03 (Terracotta).
- **Microsoft Copilot / Enterprise Hero**:
  - Headline: *"Step Beyond Theory. Lead with Executive Intelligence."*
  - Dual action CTAs with direct cohort registration and skills diagnostic.
  - Floating Copilot telemetry cards (*"AI Decision Intelligence Active"*, *"War Room Incident Command Lab"*, *"P&L Literacy"*).
- **Interactive Career & Salary ROI Calculator (`CareerRoiCalculator.jsx`)**:
  - Interactive sliders and role selectors projecting 1st-year compensation lifts and promotion acceleration.
- **Before vs. After Leadership Divide Matrix (`ComparisonMatrix.jsx`)**:
  - Side-by-side comparison of conventional college graduates vs. LAMA-trained first-time managers.
- **Azure Incident Command War Room Lab (`WarRoomSection.jsx`)**:
  - Live incident monitoring console with critical scenario simulations and command rosters.
- **Microsoft Learn-Style 8-Course Leadership Suite (`CoursesSection.jsx`)**:
  - 8 modular courses with competency badges.
- **Skills Assessment Diagnostic (`ReadinessAssessment.jsx`)**:
  - 3-step diagnostic with real-time scoring and customized tier recommendations.
- **Dual Cohort Pathways & Admissions Desk (`AdmissionsSection.jsx`)**:
  - Final-Year B.Tech / B.E. vs. MBA tracks.
  - Direct admissions desk with Chennai HQ info, phone, and email.
